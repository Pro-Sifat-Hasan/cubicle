# Brand

**Name:** Cubicle
**Tagline:** Your personal AI staff. Set them up once — they get your work done, every day.
**One-liner:** Cubicle is a free, open-source app that gives you a team of AI employees that plan, work, and report back, on your own PC.

## Voice

- Personal and calm: it's *your* staff, working for *you*.
- Speak in outcomes: "done", "needs you", "with the team".
- Light office humour is welcome, but never at the cost of clarity.

## Mark

An amber tile holding a friendly ink-coloured screen with two eyes and a smile, a desk below,
and a green "online" dot. It stands for an assistant who is always at their desk.
It is generated from code: `uv run --no-project --with pillow python scripts/brand/make_icons.py`.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Amber | `#FFB020` | Primary accent, buttons, highlights |
| Ink | `#17192B` | Text on amber, the mark |
| Mint | `#2BC48A` | Done, available, online |
| Sky | `#4C8DFF` | Working, in progress |
| Coral | `#FF5C5C` | Failed, errors |
| Slate | `#8A8FA3` | Queued, offline, muted |

Layout colours come from the Windows Fluent theme, so light and dark mode both work.
Brand colours are used only as accents.
